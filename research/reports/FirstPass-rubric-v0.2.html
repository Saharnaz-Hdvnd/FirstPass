FirstPass: rubric v0.2

The extent to which available evidence supports a listing’s identity, material claims, disclosures, and proposed transaction.

Development protocol, not a validated trust probability. The same 22 checks and proposed base weights sum to 100. This revision clarifies how decisions are made; it does not tune weights to improve the ten-car pilot’s scores. Original v0.1 records and Figma scores are preserved.

What changes now

  • Added all-elements support and explicit contradiction/unknown criteria for every check.
  • Added proposed freshness windows and material-change invalidation.
  • Added exact mileage/precision/timing reconciliation; no automatic tolerance or rollback finding.
  • Required independent seller domain/contact linkage beyond active business registry match.
  • Required upstream origin tracing for mileage and syndicated claims.
  • Separated owner-count/prior-use requirements within whole-check B4.
  • Defined visual coverage checklist and photo-attribution limits.
  • Defined full_100 and documented core_97 applicability profiles; forbid mixed-profile rankings.
  • Clarified published-price evidence versus ambiguous mandatory-fee disclaimers.
  • Added reassessment-pending/no-number display state and validation record schema.

Figma: Make version 9 still uses v0.1 cached pilot assessments. Those numbers have not been silently upgraded. A separate v0.2 queue holds all ten cases as reassessment pending; pending means no numerical v0.2 result.

State decisions

StateDecision
SupportedAll required applicable elements have traceable, adequate evidence within the rule’s scope.
ContradictedAn attributable material conflict remains after timing, units, scope and plausible record differences are reconciled.
UnknownEvidence is missing, partial, stale or unresolved. Zero supported points is not a deception finding.

No partial-credit fractions. If a combined rule proves impractical, split it with predeclared subweights in a later version rather than assigning an AI confidence percentage.

Freshness: proposed windows to test

Current listing/odometer, availability, transaction details and recall queries: 7 days. Dealer-license/title-history queries and inspection/battery tests: 30 days. Media: 30 days from an authenticated capture date. These are research design choices, not official standards or guarantees. Use each check’s designated window; a material change invalidates affected evidence immediately.

Stable VIN decoding has no arbitrary expiry, and historical events retain their dates. Access time does not authenticate a photo’s age. A dashboard clock is not an authenticated capture date. Reassess cached results before using them for a new transaction. Compare alternative windows in sensitivity analysis.

Mileage: record, reconcile, then decide

Preserve original units and precision; convert kilometres to miles using 1.609344 kilometres per mile. An explicitly rounded claim is an interval; do not invent rounding for a precise integer. Any unexplained difference remains unknown. No 5-, 10- or 100-mile forgiveness threshold was added from the observed pilot gaps.

P01’s 28,600 ad and 28,606 photo remain unresolved without authenticated reading dates. P10’s 116-mile gap also requires reconciliation. Neither alone establishes rollback. A historical timeline needs at least two dated observations and a non-current-seller upstream origin; current photo agreement belongs to C2.

Applicability and mathematics

Use full_100 for EV/hybrid battery screening, advertised certification, modification, specialized warranty/condition claims, or uncertain applicability. Use core_97 only after a complete accessible claim inventory documents an ordinary combustion listing with no applicable specialized claim. Missing descriptions are not grounds for exclusion.

In core_97, D4 is excluded with evidence and a reason, and each included weight becomes 100 × base weight / 97. Exclusion is metadata, not a fourth state. Display profile and applicable base total. Do not rank full_100 and core_97 cases together.

T = sum of normalized supported weights; D = contradicted weights; U = unknown weights; T + D + U = 100; coverage = T + D. Calculate at full precision; round only for display. Critical evidence-backed alerts take precedence over ranking. There are no good/bad trust-score thresholds.

The 22 operational checks

A1 · VIN/specification identity · 6 base points

Supported: VIN syntax/check-digit rules applicable to this VIN and compatible official decoded year/make/model/core powertrain; decode errors and known limitations resolved. Physical identity is outside this check.

Contradicted: A vehicle-attributed VIN/core specification claim conflicts with an unambiguous applicable official decode after naming conventions are reconciled.

Unknown: Decoder ambiguity, partial VIN, unresolved mismatch or missing independent decode.

Next action: Compare listing VIN/core details with official decoder; separately verify physical VIN under E1/A3.

Evidence kinds: official_vin_decode
Freshness policy: stable_vin_decode

A2 · Seller identity · 6 base points

Supported: Seller legal/DBA name, address and active applicable dealer license agree with official registry AND the listing domain/contact is independently linked through registry or manufacturer directory. A registry business-name match alone is insufficient. No phone call is required when that independent linkage exists.

Contradicted: Authoritative records establish that the claimed identity/license/domain relationship is materially false after DBA and tracking-number differences are resolved.

Unknown: Only seller-owned pages, name/address match without domain/contact linkage, unresolved group-level seller or unavailable registry.

Next action: Match registry DBA/address/license and an independent domain/contact listing. Record differing phone numbers without assuming impersonation.

Evidence kinds: official_dealer_license, independent_seller_contact
Freshness policy: dealer_license_query_days

A3 · Authority to sell · 5 base points

Supported: Current VIN-linked ownership/authorized dealer-agent chain, required transfer documents and material lien/release arrangements support the proposed seller authority, using secure verification.

Contradicted: Authenticated current owner/transfer authority conflicts with seller representation after authorized agency, inventory titling and date differences are reconciled.

Unknown: Title chain, authorization, transfer documents or applicable lien evidence missing.

Next action: Verify current title/authorized seller chain and relevant lien/release process securely; avoid retaining unnecessary personal identifiers.

Evidence kinds: title_authority_record
Freshness policy: title_history_query_days

A4 · Possession/location · 3 base points

Supported: Current dated VIN-linked vehicle/location observation independently connects the seller to possession at the stated location; ad and watermarks alone do not suffice.

Contradicted: Authenticated current VIN/location evidence materially conflicts with stated possession/location, after transit, agency and timing are resolved.

Unknown: No current attributable observation; old dealer photos or a live ad only.

Next action: Obtain dated vehicle-specific location/possession evidence or independent inspection.

Evidence kinds: current_possession_observation
Freshness policy: listing_and_current_odometer_days

B1 · Title/brand disclosure · 8 base points

Supported: Explicit current title/brand disclosure agrees with sufficiently scoped VIN-linked title/brand evidence. Record source coverage and any adverse brand; accurately disclosed branded title can pass.

Contradicted: Established relevant title/brand record conflicts with explicit disclosure, after branding/date/jurisdiction semantics are reconciled.

Unknown: No explicit disclosure, incomplete record scope, or only a clean-title boolean. A commercial report may corroborate covered events but is not represented as NMVTIS.

Next action: Compare explicit disclosure with current relevant title/brand records, noting jurisdiction, report date and exclusions.

Evidence kinds: title_brand_record
Freshness policy: title_history_query_days

B2 · Theft/total-loss/salvage disclosure · 6 base points

Supported: Explicit theft, total-loss and salvage disclosures agree with applicable scoped checks and dated vehicle-linked records; all applicable elements resolved. A single clean NICB response does not satisfy every element.

Contradicted: Covered theft/loss/salvage event materially conflicts with disclosure after event scope/date and recovery/settlement status are reconciled.

Unknown: No explicit disclosure, absent applicable checks or uncovered elements. Negative limited lookup is not an all-clear.

Next action: Record applicable insurer/history query scopes and date; compare precise claims. Respect public lookup limits; no automatic paid report.

Evidence kinds: theft_loss_salvage_record
Freshness policy: title_history_query_days

B3 · Accident/structural/repair claims · 7 base points

Supported: Explicit accident/damage/repair disclosures agree with scoped history; any reported damage has relevant repair/inspection evidence for material repair claims. A literal report-status claim may be corroborated but does not earn the whole check without required damage disclosure.

Contradicted: A material no-damage or repair assertion is contradicted by attributable history/inspection after scope/date reconciliation. Reported damage without a contrary assertion is a review question, not automatic failure.

Unknown: No disclosure, only no-events report, unresolved event/repair scope, or absolute never-damaged claim without adequate corroboration.

Next action: Distinguish reported history from actual condition; reconcile damage events, repair documentation and independent inspection scope.

Evidence kinds: damage_history_record, repair_inspection_record
Freshness policy: title_history_query_days

B4 · Ownership/prior-use claims · 4 base points

Supported: Both owner-count disclosure (including whether an estimate) AND prior-use disclosure agree with sufficient dated history; required prior-use categories include lease, rental, commercial/fleet where known.

Contradicted: A material owner-count or prior-use disclosure conflicts with sufficient history after dealer-title transfers and ownership-estimation differences are reconciled.

Unknown: One-owner claim verified but prior use omitted; owner-count flag false; incomplete history. No partial credit under whole-check rubric.

Next action: Verify owner-count method and explicit prior-use disclosure separately, then decide the combined check.

Evidence kinds: ownership_prior_use_record
Freshness policy: title_history_query_days

C1 · Mileage timeline · 8 base points

Supported: At least two dated mileage observations at different times, including one non-current-seller upstream origin, form a sufficiently informative nondecreasing sequence compatible with the listing claim after units/dates/precision are reconciled. C2 handles present photo agreement.

Contradicted: A material mileage-history claim remains incompatible with attributable dated records after units, dates, transcription and instrument replacement are checked.

Unknown: Sparse history, seller-only endpoints, uncertain dates/units, unresolved decrease or an unsupported tampering inference.

Next action: Build dated original-unit timeline; resolve decreases and source dependence; record coverage gaps without claiming completeness.

Evidence kinds: dated_mileage_history
Freshness policy: title_history_query_days

C2 · Current mileage agreement · 5 base points

Supported: Authenticated VIN-linked current odometer observation and ad/description refer to the same reading event or a documented reconciled update, with overlapping declared precision intervals; current observation within 7 days.

Contradicted: A material current reading claim conflicts with authenticated same-reference evidence after dates, units, precision and replacement are resolved.

Unknown: Any unexplained delta, missing capture date, dashboard clock alone, no VIN linkage or stale photo. No automatic small-gap tolerance.

Next action: Record exact delta and units; request dated VIN-linked observation and reconcile the reading/publication events.

Evidence kinds: current_odometer_observation
Freshness policy: listing_and_current_odometer_days

C3 · Equipment/specification claims · 4 base points

Supported: All enumerated material trim, drivetrain and option claims match suitable VIN-specific build/equipment evidence. Core identity already credited to A1 is not extra C3 support.

Contradicted: A specific material equipment/trim claim conflicts with VIN-specific build evidence or attributable actual equipment after package/model naming is resolved.

Unknown: Only core decode, generic trim defaults, unverified options, or conflicting descriptive wording.

Next action: Compare claim inventory to VIN-specific build sheet/window sticker and physical equipment as necessary.

Evidence kinds: vin_specific_equipment_record
Freshness policy: stable_vin_decode

C4 · Cross-source/current-listing consistency · 3 base points

Supported: Current availability and enumerated material claims agree across appropriately dated originating sources or an independent availability confirmation; syndicated copies recognized as one origin.

Contradicted: Same-reference material claims/availability conflict across attributable sources after source/date reconciliation.

Unknown: Only seller page plus aggregator copy, unconfirmed stock or unresolved timing. A removed ad alone does not establish deception.

Next action: Record upstream origins, timestamps and stock confirmation; do not count copies as independent checks.

Evidence kinds: independent_listing_confirmation
Freshness policy: listing_and_current_odometer_days

D1 · Condition disclosure · 5 base points

Supported: Current VIN-linked independent inspection/diagnostic scope addresses enumerated present-condition and disclosed-defect claims; relevant structural/flood/mechanical limits explicit.

Contradicted: A material condition assertion conflicts with scoped current inspection/diagnostic findings after intervening repair/date effects are resolved.

Unknown: Only photos/history, generic excellent-condition wording, missing inspection scope, outdated test or subsequent material change.

Next action: Obtain dated independent inspection and relevant diagnostics; state what was and was not examined.

Evidence kinds: independent_condition_inspection
Freshness policy: condition_and_battery_test_days

D2 · Maintenance/repair claims · 4 base points

Supported: Enumerated material maintenance/repair claims, including generic well-maintained assurance, are supported by adequate dated VIN-linked records relative to relevant service needs; reported entries have upstream provenance.

Contradicted: A specific service assertion conflicts with authenticated relevant records; absence of a receipt alone is not contradiction.

Unknown: No specific claims inventoried, unsubstantiated generic assurance, insufficient record coverage or seller assertion only.

Next action: Map each service claim to dated VIN-linked invoice/record; disclose remaining service-history gaps.

Evidence kinds: maintenance_record
Freshness policy: title_history_query_days

D3 · Safety/recall disclosure · 3 base points

Supported: Current official/manufacturer VIN-specific open-recall result and relevant completion evidence agree with explicit recall-status disclosures; lookup exclusions recorded. General model campaigns or commercial summaries alone do not satisfy this check.

Contradicted: VIN-specific unrepaired campaign materially conflicts with a no-open-recall or repair-completed assertion after update lag and repair evidence are reconciled.

Unknown: No completed current lookup, no explicit status disclosure, unresolved lag or only general warning. Open recall itself is not seller deception.

Next action: Save dated official VIN result, exclusions and applicable repair evidence; distinguish model-level from VIN-level results.

Evidence kinds: official_vin_recall_record
Freshness policy: recall_query_days

D4 · Specialized condition claims · 3 base points

Supported: For each applicable specialized area, material claims and relevant condition disclosures are supported by suitable VIN-linked evidence: battery diagnostics for EV/HEV/PHEV; actual CPO enrollment/checklist/coverage for certification; appropriate inspection for modification/special warranty claims.

Contradicted: A material specialized assertion conflicts with authenticated suitable diagnostics/enrollment/coverage evidence after test scope and dates are resolved.

Unknown: Battery charge/range display only, generic CPO boilerplate, missing VIN enrollment, insufficient warranty terms or unresolved applicability.

Next action: Obtain appropriate battery test conditions/results or VIN-linked certification, modification or warranty documentation as applicable.

Evidence kinds: specialized_condition_record
Freshness policy: condition_and_battery_test_days

E1 · Vehicle-specific media · 4 base points

Supported: Readable VIN in the reviewed photo sequence matches listing VIN and coherent vehicle views establish listing-media attribution, with no unresolved mixed/copy identity issue. This is not proof of possession or original authorship.

Contradicted: Attributable media clearly depict a different VIN/vehicle after legitimate stock illustrations and caption context are checked.

Unknown: Only filename/URL VIN, generic watermark, visually similar vehicle or unreadable physical VIN.

Next action: Inspect physical VIN image and coherent exterior/interior sequence; flag unresolved stock/copied/mixed attribution.

Evidence kinds: vehicle_vin_media
Freshness policy: vehicle_media_days

E2 · Relevant visual coverage · 3 base points

Supported: Predeclared relevant view checklist is complete and usable: all exterior sides/corners, front/rear cabin, cargo/bed as relevant, readable VIN and odometer, plus close-ups of disclosed visible defects. EV charging/battery-related visible claims and modified/certified claims need their relevant views/documents; images cannot replace diagnostics.

Contradicted: Only an explicit material assertion that all required views/evidence are supplied is demonstrably false. Missing views alone remain unknown.

Unknown: Any required view missing/obscured/unattributable. Number of photo URLs or polished presentation earns no credit.

Next action: Use checklist before looking at score; record each required view as present, missing or unusable and why.

Evidence kinds: visual_coverage_review
Freshness policy: vehicle_media_days

E3 · Media/description agreement · 3 base points

Supported: Every enumerated visually assessable material description claim matches attributable media; unobservable mechanical/structural claims excluded explicitly. VIN identity and odometer events already assigned to E1/A1/C2 add no repeated support/penalty.

Contradicted: A specific material visible claim conflicts with attributable imagery after captions, dates and variants are resolved.

Unknown: Incomplete claim inventory or attribution, limited views, unobservable assertions, unresolved mismatch.

Next action: Compare visible claim inventory to numbered images; record limits and primary rule for each event.

Evidence kinds: visual_claim_comparison
Freshness policy: vehicle_media_days

F1 · Price/terms transparency · 4 base points

Supported: Published cash purchase price, mandatory dealer charges, exclusions and material finance/trade/rebate/accessory conditions are explicit and arithmetically consistent. Published written terms suffice; a buyer order is stronger but not always required. Generic contradictory disclaimers must be reconciled.

Contradicted: Same-reference purchase-price/mandatory-charge claim conflicts with definite applicable written terms or documented quote after taxes, optional items and eligibility are reconciled.

Unknown: Unclear whether fee is included, conflicting destination/accessory boilerplate, incomplete material conditions or unresolved quote change. A low price alone is irrelevant.

Next action: Save itemized published total and conditions; obtain itemized cash quote when inclusion or eligibility is unclear.

Evidence kinds: published_price_terms
Freshness policy: listing_and_current_odometer_days

F2 · Verification cooperation · 3 base points

Supported: Documented actual seller response enables reasonable records and independent inspection, or a credible workable alternative; identities and dates tied to this listing.

Contradicted: Actual conduct establishes refusal/obstruction of reasonable verification without credible alternative after context is documented. Nonresponse alone requires context and remains unknown.

Unknown: No interaction, test-drive button only, or ambiguous/nonresponse. No automatic seller outreach.

Next action: If separately authorized, record actual response to records/inspection request; otherwise leave unknown.

Evidence kinds: observed_seller_interaction
Freshness policy: listing_and_current_odometer_days

F3 · Transaction legitimacy · 3 base points

Supported: Specific proposed legal payee, current authority, payment/escrow identity and transfer process independently verified and consistent; actual transaction proposal required.

Contradicted: Independently established impersonation, unauthorized payee/escrow or materially false transfer process conflicts with proposal after identity/context checks.

Unknown: No proposed payment/transfer details or unverified third-party assurance. Writing style, urgency alone or seller nationality is not a weighted finding.

Next action: Before any payment, independently verify named payee, authority and escrow/transfer details; preserve safe evidence, not credentials.

Evidence kinds: verified_transaction_proposal
Freshness policy: listing_and_current_odometer_days

How to use this version

  1. Keep the original ten-car evidence review as development history. Do not count it as held-out validation.
  2. For each new assessment, freeze the listing and inventory its material claims. Choose and justify the applicability profile before seeing the score.
  3. Record evidence dates, vehicle linkage, upstream origins, limitations, claim references and primary events. Apply all-elements rules without guessing missing facts.
  4. Have two reviewers independently label an overlapping sample; keep their original labels separate from adjudication. Compare agreement per check and unknown rates.
  5. After the ten-case reassessment exposes remaining ambiguities, freeze a protocol for the 50–100-vehicle feasibility dataset. Include sparse and conflicting evidence without claiming rare-fraud probability estimates.
  6. Then compare structured-only and AI-extracted claims, run the exploratory user study, and test weight/window sensitivity. Time active review with a real timer; old retrieval times are not human task times.
